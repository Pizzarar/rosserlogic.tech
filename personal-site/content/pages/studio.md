Idle Minds is my game studio. I make games built on systems that play themselves out. Two games are in development today, and both follow the same three rules.

## Studio Philosophy

- **Systemic simulations.** Games where interesting things emerge from interacting mechanics, not scripted set pieces.
- **Deterministic mechanics.** A clear, inspectable ruleset you can learn, test, and master.
- **Player-respecting pricing.** Zero ads, zero in-app purchases, zero gacha.

## Games

### Escape From Idle

A post-apocalyptic idle game with Tarkov-style extraction raids and a Melvor-style ladder that carries you from manual play to full automation. Planned at $5 flat, with no ads, IAP, or gacha. Currently in development.
